### GS²: Graph-based Spatial Distribution Optimization for Compact 3D Gaussian Splatting

3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) has demonstrated breakthrough performance in novel view synthesis and real-time rendering. Nevertheless, its practicality is constrained by the high memory cost due to a huge number of Gaussian points. Many pruning-based 3DGS variants have been proposed for memory saving, but often compromise spatial consistency and may lead to rendering artifacts. To address this issue, we propose graph-based spatial distribution optimization for compact 3D Gaussian Splatting (GS²), which enhances reconstruction quality by optimizing the spatial distribution of Gaussian points. Specifically, we introduce an evidence lower bound (ELBO)-based adaptive densification strategy that automatically controls the densification process. In addition, an opacity-aware progressive pruning strategy is proposed to further reduce memory consumption by dynamically removing low-opacity Gaussian points. Furthermore, we propose a graph-based feature encoding module to adjust the spatial distribution via feature-guided point shifting. Extensive experiments validate that GS² achieves a compact Gaussian representation while delivering superior rendering quality. Compared with 3DGS, it achieves higher PSNR with only about 12.5% Gaussian points. Furthermore, it outperforms all compared baselines in both rendering quality and memory efficiency.

3D高斯泼溅（3DGS）在新视图合成和实时渲染方面表现出突破性的性能。然而，由于高斯点数量庞大，其高昂的内存成本限制了其实用性。许多基于剪枝的3DGS变体已被提出以节省内存，但通常会牺牲空间一致性并可能导致渲染伪影。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了用于紧凑型3D高斯泼溅的基于图的空间分布优化（GS²），该方法通过优化高斯点的空间分布来增强重建质量。具体而言，我们引入了一种基于证据下界（ELBO）的自适应密集化策略，该策略自动控制密集化过程。此外，我们提出了一种不透明度感知的渐进式剪枝策略，通过动态移除低不透明度的高斯点来进一步降低内存消耗。此外，我们提出了一种基于图的特征编码模块，通过特征导向的点偏移来调整空间分布。大量实验验证了GS²在实现紧凑高斯表示的同时提供了卓越的渲染质量。与3DGS相比，它仅使用约12.5%的高斯点就实现了更高的PSNR。此外，它在渲染质量和内存效率方面均优于所有对比基线。
