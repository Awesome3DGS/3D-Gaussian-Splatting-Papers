### Pose-Free Omnidirectional Gaussian Splatting for 360-Degree Videos with Consistent Depth Priors

Omnidirectional 3D Gaussian Splatting with panoramas is a key technique for 3D scene representation, and existing methods typically rely on slow SfM to provide camera poses and sparse points priors. In this work, we propose a pose-free omnidirectional 3DGS method, named PFGS360, that reconstructs 3D Gaussians from unposed omnidirectional videos. To achieve accurate camera pose estimation, we first construct a spherical consistency-aware pose estimation module, which recovers poses by establishing consistent 2D-3D correspondences between the reconstructed Gaussians and the unposed images using Gaussians' internal depth priors. Besides, to enhance the fidelity of novel view synthesis, we introduce a depth-inlier-aware densification module to extract depth inliers and Gaussian outliers with consistent monocular depth priors, enabling efficient Gaussian densification and achieving photorealistic novel view synthesis. The experiments show significant outperformance over existing pose-free and pose-aware 3DGS methods on both real-world and synthetic 360-degree videos.

基于全景图像的全向3D高斯泼溅是3D场景表示的关键技术，现有方法通常依赖于缓慢的SfM来提供相机姿态和稀疏点先验。在这项工作中，我们提出了一种名为PFGS360的无姿态全向3DGS方法，用于从未知姿态的全向视频中重建3D高斯。为了实现精确的相机姿态估计，我们首先构建了一个球形一致性感知姿态估计模块，利用高斯的内部深度先验，在重建的高斯与未知姿态的图像之间建立一致的2D-3D对应关系，从而恢复姿态。此外，为了增强新视角合成的保真度，我们引入了一个深度内点感知的致密化模块，利用一致的单目深度先验提取深度内点和高斯离群点，从而实现高效的高斯致密化，并达到逼真的新视角合成效果。实验表明，该方法在真实世界和合成的360度视频上均显著优于现有的无姿态和有姿态3DGS方法。
