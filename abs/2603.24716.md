### Accurate Point Measurement in 3DGS -- A New Alternative to Traditional Stereoscopic-View Based Measurements

3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) has revolutionized real-time rendering with its state-of-the-art novel view synthesis, but its utility for accurate geometric measurement remains underutilized. Compared to multi-view stereo (MVS) point clouds or meshes, 3DGS rendered views present superior visual quality and completeness. However, current point measurement methods still rely on demanding stereoscopic workstations or direct picking on often-incomplete and inaccurate 3D meshes. As a novel view synthesizer, 3DGS renders exact source views and smoothly interpolates in-between views. This allows users to intuitively pick congruent points across different views while operating 3DGS models. By triangulating these congruent points, one can precisely generate 3D point measurements. This approach mimics traditional stereoscopic measurement but is significantly less demanding: it requires neither a stereo workstation nor specialized operator stereoscopic capability. Furthermore, it enables multi-view intersection (more than two views) for higher measurement accuracy. We implemented a web-based application to demonstrate this proof-of-concept (PoC). Using several UAV aerial datasets, we show this PoC allows users to successfully perform highly accurate point measurements, achieving accuracy matching or exceeding traditional stereoscopic methods on standard hardware. Specifically, our approach significantly outperforms direct mesh-based measurements. Quantitatively, our method achieves RMSEs in the 1-2 cm range on well-defined points. More critically, on challenging thin structures where mesh-based RMSE was 0.062 m, our method achieved 0.037 m. On sharp corners poorly reconstructed in the mesh, our method successfully measured all points with a 0.013 m RMSE, whereas the mesh method failed entirely.

3D高斯泼溅（3DGS）利用其最先进的新视图合成彻底变革了实时渲染，但其在精确几何测量方面的应用仍未得到充分利用。与多视图立体（MVS）点云或网格相比，3DGS渲染的视图具有卓越的视觉质量和完整性。然而，当前的点测量方法仍然依赖于要求苛刻的立体工作站，或者在通常不完整且不准确的3D网格上进行直接拾取。作为一种新视图合成器，3DGS渲染精确的源视图并在视图之间平滑插值。这使得用户在操作3DGS模型时，能够直观地在不同视图中选取对应点。通过对这些对应点进行三角测量，可以精确生成3D点测量数据。这种方法模仿了传统的立体测量，但要求显著降低：它既不需要立体工作站，也不需要操作员具备专门的立体观察能力。此外，它支持多视图相交（超过两个视图）以获得更高的测量精度。我们实现了一个基于Web的应用程序来演示这个概念验证。使用几个无人机航拍数据集，我们展示了该概念验证允许用户在标准硬件上成功执行高精度的点测量，达到了匹配或超过传统立体测量方法的精度。具体而言，我们的方法显著优于基于网格的直接测量。定量来看，我们的方法在特征明确的点上实现了1-2厘米范围内的均方根误差（RMSE）。更关键的是，在基于网格的RMSE为0.062米的具有挑战性的细长结构上，我们的方法达到了0.037米。在网格重建效果较差的尖锐拐角处，我们的方法成功测量了所有点，RMSE为0.013米，而网格方法则完全失败。
