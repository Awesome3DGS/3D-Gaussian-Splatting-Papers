### Fake3DGS: A Benchmark for 3D Manipulation Detection in Neural Rendering

Recent advances in 3D reconstruction and neural rendering,particularly 3D Gaussian Splatting, make it feasible and simple to edit 3D scenes and re-render them as highly realistic images. Therefore, security concerns arise regarding the authenticity of 3D content. Despite this threat, 3D fake detection remains largely unexplored in the literature, and most existing work is limited to 2D space. Therefore, in this paper, we formalize the concept of 3D fake detection and introduce Fake3DGS, a dataset of 3D Gaussian splatting scenes and corresponding rendered views, where fake images are produced by controlled manipulations of geometry, appearance, and spatial layout, while preserving high visual realism. Using this benchmark, we demonstrate that current state-of-the-art 2D detectors struggle to distinguish between original and 3D manipulated images. To bridge this gap, we introduce a 3D-aware detection method that leverages multi-view coherence and features derived from the Gaussian splatting representation. Experimental results demonstrate a substantial improvement in recognizing modified 3D content, underscoring the validity of the new dataset and the necessity for authenticity assessment techniques that extend beyond 2D evidence.

3D重建和神经渲染（特别是3D高斯泼溅，3D Gaussian Splatting）的最新进展使得编辑3D场景并将其重新渲染为高度逼真的图像变得可行且简单。因此，关于3D内容真实性的安全问题随之产生。尽管存在这种威胁，但在文献中对3D虚假内容的检测仍然缺乏探索，而大多数现有工作仅限于2D空间。因此，在本文中，我们形式化了3D虚假检测的概念，并介绍了Fake3DGS，这是一个包含3D高斯泼溅场景及相应渲染视图的数据集，其中虚假图像是通过对几何、外观和空间布局进行受控操纵生成的，同时保持了高度的视觉真实感。利用该基准，我们证明了当前最先进的2D检测器难以区分原始图像和3D操纵图像。为了弥补这一差距，我们引入了一种3D感知检测方法，该方法利用了多视图一致性和从高斯泼溅表示中导出的特征。实验结果表明，在识别修改后的3D内容方面有显著改进，强调了新数据集的有效性以及超越2D证据的真实性评估技术的必要性。
