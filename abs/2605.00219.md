### VkSplat: High-Performance 3DGS Training in Vulkan Compute

We present VkSplat, a high-performance, cross-vendor 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) training pipeline implemented fully in Vulkan compute, addressing performance and compatibility limitation of existing training pipelines. With various optimizations, we achieve 3.3× speed and 33% VRAM reduction over CUDA+PyTorch baseline, maintaining quality, and demonstrating compatibility across GPU vendors. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first fully-Vulkan-based 3DGS training pipeline that achieves state-of-the-art performance.

我们提出了 VkSplat，这是一个完全在 Vulkan 计算中实现的高性能、跨供应商的 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) 训练流水线，解决了现有训练流水线的性能和兼容性限制。通过各种优化，我们实现了比 CUDA+PyTorch 基准快 3.3 倍的速度和 33% 的显存（VRAM）减少，同时保持了质量，并展示了跨 GPU 供应商的兼容性。据我们所知，这是第一个实现最先进性能的完全基于 Vulkan 的 3DGS 训练流水线。
