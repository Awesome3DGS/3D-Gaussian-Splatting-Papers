### AA-Splat: Anti-Aliased Feed-forward Gaussian Splatting

Feed-forward 3D Gaussian Splatting (FF-3DGS) emerges as a fast and robust solution for sparse-view 3D reconstruction and novel view synthesis (NVS). However, existing FF-3DGS methods are built on incorrect screen-space dilation filters, causing severe rendering artifacts when rendering at out-of-distribution sampling rates. We firstly propose an FF-3DGS model, called AA-Splat, to enable robust anti-aliased rendering at any resolution. AA-Splat utilizes an opacity-balanced band-limiting (OBBL) design, which combines two components: a 3D band-limiting post-filter integrates multi-view maximal frequency bounds into the feed-forward reconstruction pipeline, effectively band-limiting the resulting 3D scene representations and eliminating degenerate Gaussians; an Opacity Balancing (OB) to seamlessly integrate all pixel-aligned Gaussian primitives into the rendering process, compensating for the increased overlap between expanded Gaussian primitives. AA-Splat demonstrates drastic improvements with average 5.4~7.5dB PSNR gains on NVS performance over a state-of-the-art (SOTA) baseline, DepthSplat, at all resolutions, between 4× and 1/4×.

前馈3D高斯泼溅（FF-3DGS）作为一种快速且稳健的解决方案，适用于稀疏视角3D重建和新视角合成（NVS）。然而，现有的FF-3DGS方法构建在不正确的屏幕空间膨胀过滤器之上，当在分布外采样率下渲染时，会导致严重的渲染伪影。我们首先提出了一个名为AA-Splat的FF-3DGS模型，以实现任意分辨率下的稳健抗锯齿渲染。AA-Splat利用了不透明度平衡带限（OBBL）设计，该设计结合了两个组件：一个3D带限后置过滤器将多视图最大频率边界集成到前馈重建管道中，有效地对生成的3D场景表示进行带限处理并消除退化高斯；一种不透明度平衡（OB）方法，将所有像元对齐的高斯图元无缝集成到渲染过程中，补偿扩展后的高斯图元之间增加的重叠。AA-Splat在从4倍到1/4倍的所有分辨率下，相比最先进（SOTA）基线DepthSplat，在NVS性能上显示出显著改进，平均PSNR增益为5.4至7.5dB。
