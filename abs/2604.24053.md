### Light 'em Up: Enabling Few-Shot Low-Light 3D Gaussian Splatting with Multi-Scale Explicit Retinex Illumination Decoupling

Full 360° novel view synthesis under low-light conditions remains challenging. Insufficient illumination, noise amplification, and view-dependent photometric inconsistencies prevent existing methods from jointly preserving geometric consistency and photorealism. Unsupervised approaches often exhibit color drift under large viewpoint variations, while supervised low-light enhancement models, though effective for 2D tasks, struggle to generalize to new scenes and typically require retraining. To address this issue, we propose MERID-GS, a Multi-Scale Explicit Retinex Illumination-Decoupled Gaussian framework for low-light 360° synthesis. Based on Retinex theory, the method explicitly separates illumination and reflectance, and suppresses noise propagation while enhancing dark-region structures via a learnable gain and Illumination-State-Guided Frequency Gating. Combined with lightweight Reflection Head and 3D Gaussian Splatting, MERID-GS adapts to new scenes with only a few shots and enables stable low-light novel view synthesis from sparse-view observations. In addition, we construct a low-light multi-view dataset covering full 360° scenes for joint evaluation. Thorough experiments across multiple datasets in this area demonstrate that MERID-GS achieves SOTA performance, exhibiting superior cross-scene generalization and view consistency.

在低光照条件下进行全360度新视图合成仍然具有挑战性。光照不足、噪声放大以及视图相关的光度不一致性阻碍了现有方法同时保持几何一致性和逼真度。无监督方法在大视角变化下经常出现颜色漂移，而监督式低光增强模型虽然在2D任务中有效，但难以泛化到新场景，通常需要重新训练。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了MERID-GS，这是一种用于低光360度合成的多尺度显式Retinex光照解耦高斯框架。基于Retinex理论，该方法显式分离了光照和反射率，并通过可学习的增益和光照状态引导的频率门控在增强暗部区域结构的同时抑制噪声传播。结合轻量级反射头和3D高斯泼溅，MERID-GS仅需几次拍摄即可适应新场景，并能从稀疏视图观测中实现稳定的低光新视图合成。此外，我们构建了一个覆盖全360度场景的低光多视图数据集，用于联合评估。在该领域的多个数据集上进行的详尽实验表明，MERID-GS实现了最先进的（SOTA）性能，表现出卓越的跨场景泛化能力和视图一致性。
