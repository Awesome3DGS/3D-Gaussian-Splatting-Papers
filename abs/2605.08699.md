### Thin-Client Interactive Gaussian Adaptive Streaming over HTTP/3

Recent advancements in 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) have enabled photorealistic rendering of complex scenes, yet widespread adoption on mobile and Extended Reality (XR) devices is hindered by substantial computational and bandwidth requirements. While existing solutions often focus on model compression for client-side rendering, they still demand significant GPU power, limiting applicability on resource-constrained hardware. We propose TIGAS (Thin-client Interactive Gaussian Adaptive Streaming), a remote rendering framework offloading rasterization to a backend. To bypass the prohibitive latencies connected to fluctuating network conditions, TIGAS streams view-dependent 2D projections to a lightweight web client over QUIC, minimizing head-of-line (HoL) blocking. A dedicated ABR algorithm adapts rendering quality to fluctuating network conditions, maintaining motion-to-photon latency within strict 6DoF interactive constraints. Furthermore, we discuss the integration of an experimental WebGPU super-resolution pipeline to analyze the trade-offs between perceptual quality enhancements and thin-client processing bottlenecks. We extensively evaluate TIGAS across multi-continental environments using 14 3DGS models and real 6DoF EyeNavGS movement traces. Powered by a backend rendering frames in under 10 milliseconds, TIGAS maintains latency within interactive thresholds while achieving an average SSIM of 0.88, serving both as a robust testbed for 3DGS streaming research and a capable delivery system.

3D高斯泼溅 (3DGS) 的最新进展已经实现了对复杂场景的逼真渲染，然而，巨大的计算和带宽需求阻碍了其在移动和扩展现实 (XR) 设备上的广泛采用。虽然现有的解决方案通常侧重于用于客户端渲染的模型压缩，但它们仍然需要大量的GPU算力，限制了其在资源受限硬件上的适用性。我们提出了 TIGAS（瘦客户端交互式高斯自适应流传输），这是一个将光栅化卸载到后端的远程渲染框架。为了绕过与波动的网络条件相关的过高延迟，TIGAS 通过 QUIC 将依赖视角的 2D 投影流式传输到一个轻量级 Web 客户端，从而最大限度地减少了队头 (HoL) 阻塞。一个专用的 ABR 算法根据波动的网络条件调整渲染质量，将运动到光子的延迟保持在严格的 6DoF 交互约束范围内。此外，我们讨论了实验性 WebGPU 超分辨率管道的集成，以分析感知质量增强与瘦客户端处理瓶颈之间的权衡。我们在跨洲际环境中，利用 14 个 3DGS 模型和真实的 6DoF EyeNavGS 移动轨迹对 TIGAS 进行了广泛的评估。得益于后端能在 10 毫秒内渲染帧，TIGAS 将延迟保持在交互阈值内，同时平均 SSIM 达到 0.88，既作为 3DGS 流媒体研究的稳健测试平台，也是一个有能力的交付系统。
