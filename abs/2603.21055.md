### SGAD-SLAM: Splatting Gaussians at Adjusted Depth for Better Radiance Fields in RGBD SLAM

3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) has made remarkable progress in RGBD SLAM. Current methods usually use 3D Gaussians or view-tied 3D Gaussians to represent radiance fields in tracking and mapping. However, these Gaussians are either too flexible or too limited in movements, resulting in slow convergence or limited rendering quality. To resolve this issue, we adopt pixel-aligned Gaussians but allow each Gaussian to adjust its position along its ray to maximize the rendering quality, even if Gaussians are simplified to improve system scalability. To speed up the tracking, we model the depth distribution around each pixel as a Gaussian distribution, and then use these distributions to align each frame to the 3D scene quickly. We report our evaluations on widely used benchmarks, justify our designs, and show advantages over the latest methods in view rendering, camera tracking, runtime, and storage complexity.

3D高斯泼溅（3DGS）在RGBD SLAM方面取得了显著进展。当前的方法通常使用3D高斯或视图绑定的3D高斯来表示跟踪和建图中的辐射场。然而，这些高斯要么过于灵活，要么在运动上过于受限，导致收敛速度慢或渲染质量有限。为了解决这个问题，我们采用了像素对齐的高斯，但允许每个高斯沿着其光线调整位置以最大化渲染质量，即使为了提高系统的可扩展性简化了高斯也是如此。为了加快跟踪速度，我们将每个像素周围的深度分布建模为高斯分布，然后使用这些分布将每一帧快速对齐到3D场景中。我们在广泛使用的基准测试上报告了评估结果，论证了我们的设计，并展示了在视图渲染、相机跟踪、运行时间和存储复杂度方面相比最新方法的优势。
