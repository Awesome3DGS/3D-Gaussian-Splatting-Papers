### Splaxel: Efficient Distributed Training of 3D Gaussian Splatting for Large-scale Scene Reconstruction via Pixel-level Communication

3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) enables high-fidelity and real-time 3D scene reconstruction, but scaling training to large-scale scenes requires optimizing hundreds of millions of Gaussians across multiple GPUs. Existing distributed approaches either partition scenes into isolated regions, causing global inconsistency, or rely on global Gaussian-level exchanges, which lead to substantial growth in inter-GPU communication and quickly dominate iteration time. We propose Splaxel, a communication-efficient distributed 3DGS training framework based on pixel-level local rendering and global composition. Instead of synchronizing Gaussians, each GPU renders its local subset and exchanges only partial pixel values, maintaining mathematical consistency while keeping communication cost stable as the scene size increases. Splaxel further reduces pixel-level redundancy through geometric and transmittance visibility prediction and improves GPU utilization via conflict-free camera-view consolidation. Evaluated on large-scale datasets with up to 120M Gaussians, Splaxel achieves up to 7.6× speedup over the state-of-the-art distributed 3DGS framework while preserving high reconstruction quality.

3D 高斯泼溅（3DGS）能够实现高保真、实时的三维场景重建，但将训练扩展到大规模场景需要在多个 GPU 上优化数亿个高斯。现有分布式方法要么将场景划分为相互独立的区域，导致全局不一致；要么依赖全局高斯级交换，造成 GPU 间通信量大幅增长，并迅速主导每次迭代的耗时。我们提出 Splaxel，一种基于像素级局部渲染与全局合成的通信高效分布式 3DGS 训练框架。各 GPU 渲染其本地高斯子集，仅交换部分像素值，从而在保持数学一致性的同时，使通信成本在场景规模增大时保持稳定。Splaxel 进一步通过几何与透射率可见性预测减少像素级冗余，并通过无冲突的相机视图整合提高 GPU 利用率。在包含多达 1.2 亿个高斯的大规模数据集上，Splaxel 在保持高重建质量的同时，相较于最先进的分布式 3DGS 框架实现了最高 7.6 倍的加速。
