### GHOST: Fast Category-agnostic Hand-Object Interaction Reconstruction from RGB Videos using Gaussian Splatting

Understanding realistic hand-object interactions from monocular RGB videos is essential for AR/VR, robotics, and embodied AI. Existing methods rely on category-specific templates or heavy computation, yet still produce physically inconsistent hand-object alignment in 3D. We introduce GHOST (Gaussian Hand-Object Splatting), a fast, category-agnostic framework for reconstructing dynamic hand-object interactions using 2D Gaussian Splatting. GHOST represents both hands and objects as dense, view-consistent Gaussian discs and introduces three key innovations: (1) a geometric-prior retrieval and consistency loss that completes occluded object regions, (2) a grasp-aware alignment that refines hand translations and object scale to ensure realistic contact, and (3) a hand-aware background loss that prevents penalizing hand-occluded object regions. GHOST achieves complete, physically consistent, and animatable reconstructions from a single RGB video while running an order of magnitude faster than prior category-agnostic methods. Extensive experiments on ARCTIC, HO3D, and in-the-wild datasets demonstrate state-of-the-art accuracy in 3D reconstruction and 2D rendering quality, establishing GHOST as an efficient and robust solution for realistic hand-object interaction modeling.

从单目RGB视频理解逼真的手物交互对于AR/VR、机器人和具身智能至关重要。现有方法依赖于特定类别的模板或繁重的计算，但仍然会产生物理上不一致的3D手物体对齐。我们介绍了GHOST（Gaussian Hand-Object Splatting），这是一个使用2D高斯泼溅（Gaussian Splatting）重建动态手物交互的快速、与类别无关的框架。GHOST将手和物体都表示为密集的、视角一致的高斯圆盘（Gaussian discs），并引入了三个关键创新：(1) 几何先验检索和一致性损失，用于完成被遮挡的物体区域；(2) 抓取感知对齐，用于细化手部平移和物体缩放以确保真实的接触；(3) 手部感知背景损失，用于防止对被手遮挡的物体区域进行惩罚。GHOST可以从单个RGB视频实现完整的、物理一致的且可动画化的重建，同时运行速度比先前的与类别无关的方法快一个数量级。在ARCTIC、HO3D和自然场景(in-the-wild)数据集上进行的广泛实验证明了GHOST在3D重建和2D渲染质量方面具有最先进的精度，确立了GHOST作为逼真手物交互建模的高效且鲁棒的解决方案。
