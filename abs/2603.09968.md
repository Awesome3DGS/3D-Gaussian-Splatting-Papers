### ReCoSplat: Autoregressive Feed-Forward Gaussian Splatting Using Render-and-Compare

Online novel view synthesis remains challenging, requiring robust scene reconstruction from sequential, often unposed, observations. We present ReCoSplat, an autoregressive feed-forward Gaussian Splatting model supporting posed or unposed inputs, with or without camera intrinsics. While assembling local Gaussians using camera poses scales better than canonical-space prediction, it creates a dilemma during training: using ground-truth poses ensures stability but causes a distribution mismatch when predicted poses are used at inference. To address this, we introduce a Render-and-Compare (ReCo) module. ReCo renders the current reconstruction from the predicted viewpoint and compares it with the incoming observation, providing a stable conditioning signal that compensates for pose errors. To support long sequences, we propose a hybrid KV cache compression strategy combining early-layer truncation with chunk-level selective retention, reducing the KV cache size by over 90% for 100+ frames. ReCoSplat achieves state-of-the-art performance across different input settings on both in- and out-of-distribution benchmarks.

在线新视角合成仍然充满挑战，因为它需要从连续且常常未定姿态的观测中进行鲁棒的场景重建。我们提出 ReCoSplat，这是一种自回归前馈的 Gaussian Splatting 模型，支持已知或未知位姿输入，并且可在有无相机内参的条件下工作。虽然利用相机位姿组装局部高斯比在规范空间中直接预测具有更好的可扩展性，但训练时会产生两难：使用真实位姿可保证稳定性，却会在推理阶段改用预测位姿时导致分布不匹配。为此，我们引入 Render-and-Compare（ReCo）模块。ReCo 从预测视角渲染当前重建结果，并与新到达的观测进行比较，提供稳定的条件信号以补偿位姿误差。为支持长序列，我们提出一种混合 KV 缓存压缩策略，将早期层截断与按块选择性保留结合起来，在 100 帧以上序列中将 KV 缓存大小减少超过 90%。在分布内和分布外基准上，ReCoSplat 在不同输入设定下都达到了最先进性能。
