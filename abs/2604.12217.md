### VVGT: Visual Volume-Grounded Transformer

Volumetric visualization has long been dominated by Direct Volume Rendering (DVR), which operates on dense voxel grids and suffers from limited scalability as resolution and interactivity demands increase. Recent advances in 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) offer a representation-centric alternative; however, existing volumetric extensions still depend on costly per-scene optimization, limiting scalability and interactivity. We present VVGT (Visual Volume-Grounded Transformer), a feed-forward, representation-first framework that directly maps volumetric data to a 3D Gaussian Splatting representation, advancing a new paradigm for volumetric visualization beyond DVR. Unlike prior feed-forward 3DGS methods designed for surface-centric reconstruction, VVGT explicitly accounts for volumetric rendering, where each pixel aggregates contributions along a ray. VVGT employs a dual-transformer network and introduces Volume Geometry Forcing, an epipolar cross-attention mechanism that integrates multi-view observations into distributed 3D Gaussian primitives without surface assumptions. This design eliminates per-scene optimization while enabling accurate volumetric representations. Extensive experiments show that VVGT achieves high-quality visualization with orders-of-magnitude faster conversion, improved geometric consistency, and strong zero-shot generalization across diverse datasets, enabling truly interactive and scalable volumetric visualization.

体可视化长期以来一直由直接体渲染（DVR）主导，该技术在密集体素网格上运行，并随着分辨率和交互性需求的增加而受到可扩展性有限的困扰。3D高斯泼溅（3DGS）的最新进展提供了一种以表征为核心的替代方案；然而，现有的体积扩展方法仍然依赖昂贵的每场景优化，限制了可扩展性和交互性。我们提出了VVGT（Visual Volume-Grounded Transformer），这是一个前馈的、表征优先的框架，直接将体积数据映射到3D高斯泼溅表示，推动了超越DVR的体可视化新范式。与之前专为以表面为中心的重建而设计的前馈3DGS方法不同，VVGT显式地考虑了体渲染，其中每个像素沿光线聚合贡献。VVGT采用双Transformer网络，并引入了体积几何强制（Volume Geometry Forcing），这是一种极线交叉注意力机制，可在没有表面假设的情况下将多视图观测整合到分布式的3D高斯基元中。这种设计消除了每场景优化，同时实现了精确的体积表示。大量实验表明，VVGT实现了高质量的可视化，转换速度提高了数量级，几何一致性得到改善，并且在跨不同数据集时具有强大的零样本泛化能力，从而实现了真正交互式和可扩展的体可视化。
